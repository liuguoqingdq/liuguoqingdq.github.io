About Me

I am an M.S. student at the School of Cyberspace Security, Guangzhou University . I am also a Research Assistant at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen (HITSZ) , mentored by Siqi Cai.

In parallel with my academic research, I work as an Engineer and Senior Partner at Guangzhou Baishida Network Technology Co., Ltd. , where I am involved in the engineering and development of AI-enabled recruitment systems and related intelligent infrastructure.

My current research focuses on efficient artificial intelligence systems, especially large language model inference, speculative decoding, high-throughput LLM serving, and GPU-efficient execution.

I am particularly interested in bridging machine learning algorithms and systems optimization, with an emphasis on throughput, memory efficiency, scalability, and practical deployment efficiency.

I also have experience in edge AI deployment, including deploying neural translation models on NVIDIA Jetson AGX for a real-time smart translation glasses system.

Research Interests
