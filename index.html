About Me

I am an M.S. student at the School of Cyberspace Security, Guangzhou University . I am also a Research Assistant at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen (HITSZ) , mentored by Siqi Cai.

My research focuses on efficient artificial intelligence systems, especially large language model inference, speculative decoding, high-throughput LLM serving, and GPU-efficient execution.

I am particularly interested in improving the throughput, memory efficiency, and scalability of large language model inference systems through algorithm-system co-design.

Research Interests

Efficient LLM Inference Inference acceleration, decoding optimization, and efficient execution for large language models.
Speculative Decoding Parallel drafting, candidate selection, verification, and resource-efficient speculative inference.
LLM Serving Systems High-throughput, memory-efficient, and scalable serving for large language models.
GPU & AI Systems GPU runtime optimization, memory management, scheduling, and high-performance AI infrastructure.
